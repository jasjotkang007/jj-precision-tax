Year-End Tax-Planning Checklist

Use this checklist to organize documents and identify tax questions before the end of the calendar year. Not every item applies to every taxpayer. This is a general information tool, not personalized tax advice.

Timing matters: Some items need to be completed before December 31, while RRSP contributions for a prior tax year may be made during the first 60 days of the following year. FHSA contributions generally count in the calendar year made. Verify the official dates and your available room before acting.

Keep records accessible and secure

Keep Notices of Assessment, tax slips, contribution receipts, invoices, receipts, mileage logs, bank records and CRA correspondence for the applicable retention period. Use secure methods to share sensitive information; never share CRA passwords or multi-factor authentication codes.
