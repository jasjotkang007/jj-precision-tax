Common Tax Questions

Do I get a refund just because I paid tuition fees?

Not necessarily. Tuition generally creates a non-refundable credit that can reduce tax payable. Eligible unused amounts may generally be carried forward or transferred where allowed.

Can I claim moving expenses when I move to another city?

Not just because you moved. A qualifying move generally must bring your new home at least 40 kilometres closer to a new work location or school, and other conditions apply.

Can every employee deduct home-office expenses?

No. Eligibility depends on the work arrangement, expenses paid and CRA conditions. Form T2200 is commonly relevant.

Why might benefits change after I get married or move?

Benefits can depend on family income, marital status, residence and other eligibility rules. Report changes promptly.
