CANADIAN TAX ESTIMATE

Income Tax & Refund
Calculator.

A basic educational estimate for 2024, 2025 or 2026, with common income sources, income tax deducted at source, the basic personal amount and the employment amount.

Privacy first: This calculator runs in your browser. Figures are not sent to JJ Precision or stored in a database. Do not enter your name, SIN, address or banking information.

Income sources

Enter annual amounts. For dividends, use the taxable amounts shown on the return. For capital gains, enter the taxable capital-gain amount.

Deductions, credits and tax already paid

For example, RRSP deductions or eligible moving expenses.
Use the eligible amount shown on your T2202. This is not the amount of tuition fees you get back.
Check your latest notice of assessment. Do not include an amount already transferred to another person.
Enter qualifying unreimbursed expenses for the applicable claim period. The calculator applies a simplified income threshold.
Optional rough estimate for a single person with no eligible dependants. CWB eligibility and amounts depend on residency, working income, family status and other rules; verify before relying on it.

Your estimate

Enter your figures and calculate.

Important: This is a simplified prototype, not tax-preparation software. It estimates basic federal and provincial/territorial income tax using general brackets and selected non-refundable credits. It does not fully model dividend tax credits, EI, Quebec abatement, surtaxes, all provincial credits, AMT, income-tested benefits or special rules. Self-employment CPP is estimated for simple cases and may be wrong if you also had employment income, CPP contributions on T4 slips, or other CPP adjustments. CWB is only approximated for a 2026 single-person scenario when selected; other family situations are not estimated. Tuition and medical credits are simplified. Rates and thresholds must be checked for the selected tax year before relying on this estimate. Quebec residents generally file a separate provincial return.
