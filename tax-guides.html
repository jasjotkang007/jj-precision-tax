Popular Tax Questions

Does a tax deduction mean I get that amount back?

No. A deduction generally reduces the income on which tax is calculated. The tax savings depend on your situation and marginal tax rate. A credit works differently and may reduce tax payable, but not every credit is refundable.

Why can I owe tax even when tax was deducted from my pay?

Payroll deductions are estimates based on information available to your employer. Having multiple jobs, other income, taxable benefits, deductions or credits can change the final calculation. Compare your full-year income and deductions when filing.

Do I need to file a tax return if I had little or no income?

Filing may still be useful or necessary. It can help determine eligibility for benefits and credits, preserve or calculate certain tax attributes, and meet filing obligations. Requirements depend on your circumstances.

When should I update CRA about a life change?

Update relevant information as soon as required by CRA rules. Marital status, address, custody and residency changes may affect benefits, notices or correspondence. Keep supporting records and review the official instructions for the specific change.

Can I rely on an online calculator for my final refund?

Use a calculator as an estimate only. Results depend on the information entered and the calculator's assumptions; it may not account for all credits, deductions, benefits, payroll overpayments or special circumstances.
