A Simple Pre-Filing Checklist

Use these prompts to prepare; they are not a complete list for every taxpayer.

  • Gather all income slips and records of other income.
  • Collect receipts and supporting documents for deductions and credits you may claim.
  • Check tuition carryforward, RRSP deduction room and FHSA records where applicable.
  • Update relevant CRA details for address, marital status, dependants and direct deposit.
  • For business income, reconcile books to bank activity and review GST/HST and payroll accounts.
  • Review prior-year notices of assessment, instalment requirements and outstanding CRA correspondence.
  • Confirm filing and payment deadlines for your situation.

See the detailed year-end checklist →

Planning note: Do not make a contribution, withdrawal, transfer or change solely because of a general article. Check eligibility, available room, timing and the tax treatment for your circumstances.
