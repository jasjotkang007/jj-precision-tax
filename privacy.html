Privacy Policy.

How this website handles information.

Contact information

If you contact us through the website, we may receive the name, email, phone and message you choose to submit. Do not send your SIN, bank information or tax documents through a general contact form.

Calculator entries

The calculators are designed to run in your browser. Their code does not send entries to JJ Precision, store them in a JJ Precision database or link them to your identity. Avoid entering identifying information. Your browser/device may retain information temporarily.

Third-party services

Hosting, email and linked websites may have their own privacy practices. Review those policies before sharing information.
