How it works

The calculator applies the tax rate you enter to the additional overtime pay, then subtracts the extra payroll deductions you enter. It does not calculate your full annual tax, determine your actual marginal rate, or automatically model benefit changes or CPP/EI maximums.

Important: Your actual tax rate depends on total annual taxable income and province or territory. Payroll withholding on an overtime cheque may look high or low compared with your final tax result. This tool is a rough illustration, not a replacement for a full-year tax estimate.
